## Description

```
DO YOU KNOW WHEN YOU'LL RUN OUT OF CASH?

Most online sellers know their revenue. Almost nobody knows how much will be in
the bank in four weeks — after the next stock order is paid for. That's the number
FlowStock works out.

You drop in your sales export as a CSV. That's it. No account, no sign-up,
no subscription, no cloud.

── WHAT YOU GET ────────────────────────

• A balance forecast up to 180 days ahead, including the restock orders that
  fall due inside that window
• A restock plan: which product to order by when, how many units, and what it costs
• A sell-out warning per product, derived from sales velocity and supplier lead time
• Gross profit per product — after fees, shipping and cost of goods
• Revenue by sales channel (Etsy, Shopify, eBay, Amazon, your own shop)
• Capital tied up in stock, at a glance
• Every table exports to CSV, the dashboard prints to PDF

── HOW IT WORKS ────────────────────────

1. Double-click the file to open it in your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari)
2. Drop in your sales CSV — columns are detected automatically
3. Optionally add your stock list, and FlowStock plans your reorders too
4. Enter your opening balance and fixed costs — done

Common column names from Etsy, Shopify, eBay and Amazon exports are already
recognised. Both English and German number and date formats are understood.

── YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU ────────────

FlowStock is a single HTML file. The entire analysis runs in JavaScript inside
your browser. There is no server, no database, no upload and no tracker. You can
switch off your Wi-Fi and keep working. That isn't a marketing promise, it's a
technical property: with no internet connection the file cannot send anything
anywhere.

── INCLUDED IN THE DOWNLOAD ────────────

• FlowStock.html — the complete tool, one file
• 4 sample CSVs (sales + stock, English and German)
• Quick-start guide as PDF, English and German
• License terms

── LANGUAGES ───────────────────────────

English and German, switchable with one click — even mid-analysis.
Currency selectable: EUR, USD, GBP, CHF, SEK, PLN, CAD, AUD.

── REQUIREMENTS ────────────────────────

A current browser on Windows, macOS, Linux, iPad or an Android tablet.
No Excel, no Google account, no installation, no internet needed.
It works on a phone, but a larger screen is a lot more pleasant.

── PLEASE NOTE ─────────────────────────

This is an instant digital download. Nothing is shipped. Digital files can't be
returned once downloaded — message me beforehand if you're unsure whether your
export will fit. I'm happy to look at your column names first.

FlowStock extrapolates from your past sales. Seasonality, promotions, marketplace
payout delays and taxes are not modelled. It is not accounting software and not
tax advice.

Questions? Message me on Etsy. I usually reply within a day.
```

## Shop FAQ entries

**Does this work with my Etsy export?**
Yes. In your Etsy shop go to Shop Manager → Settings → Options → Download Data and
download the "Order Items" file. The columns are recognised automatically.

**Do I need Excel?**
No. FlowStock is an HTML file and runs in your browser.

**Is my sales data sent anywhere?**
No. The file contains no network calls at all. You can use it offline.

**Do I get updates?**
Yes, free of charge. Message me on Etsy after an update and I'll send you the new file.

**Can I use it for several shops?**
Yes — the license covers you and your business, across as many of your own shops
and devices as you like. Passing the file to third parties is not permitted.
